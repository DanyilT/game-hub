# GameHub in Storybook

Every page and part of the site, drawn by the site's own code. Only the world outside the page is pretend:

- **Supabase** (accounts, friends, ratings, play time, records) answers from a made-up world of players
  (`dev/storybook/mocks/data.js`). What you change in a story (a favorite, a block, a rating) lasts until the story is
  drawn again.
- **The game in the frame** is the real game, from its own site, but it can't talk to Storybook: what it would report
  (connected, its numbers, the save, the download, a replay) comes from the story's controls instead.
- **Replays** come from the story, not from this browser's storage.

## The toolbar

| | |
|---|---|
| **Account** | Signed in (Dany), a new player (no username picked yet: the welcome window asks), a guest, or a site without accounts. |
| **Network** | Online or offline, as the site sees it (`navigator.onLine`): offline toasts, tickets left, dimmed games. |
| **Server** | How the pretend Supabase answers: fast, slow (2 s), down (every request fails), or never (everything keeps loading). |
| **Navigation** | The sidebar, or the floating button in either corner (Settings → Preferences). |

The viewport button (top left of the toolbar) shows any story at a phone or tablet width.

## Where to look

- **Pages**: the whole site, layout and all, at each address. Their controls change what's on them: the game page's
  controls set the game, its panel, the save and download, offline tickets, a replay, and a message at the bottom of
  the screen.
- **Design system**: what every page is built from: the one `Button` (each variant, tone, size and shape, and what it
  can do), the game's `Tag`, `Badge`, the form fields, messages and statuses, the page title, `Disclosure`, `Tabs`,
  `Popover`, and the surfaces content sits on (cards, panels, windows, popups).
- **Layout, Game, Account, Common**: each part on its own, with its states.
- Stories that open something (a menu, a window, the filters) do it by clicking, like a player would: see the
  **Interactions** tab.
- The **Accessibility** tab checks each story.

## Adding a story

Stories live in `dev/storybook/stories`, never next to the site's code. A page story renders `<AppRoutes />` with
`parameters.route`; `parameters.scenario` (an object, or a function of the story's controls) sets what the pretend
world says. See `dev/storybook/README.md`.
